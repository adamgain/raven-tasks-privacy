Privacy Policy for Raven Tasks

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Overview

Raven Tasks is a task and productivity app developed by Adam Gain. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data Collection

Everything you enter into the app — tasks, reminders, mood logs, hyperfocus notes, journal entries, and any other content — is stored entirely on your own device, in a local database. None of this ever leaves your device, and the developer has no access to it.

The one exception is the optional in-app Feedback form (Settings → Help & Feedback). If you choose to submit feedback, the following is sent to a Google Form the developer uses to collect and review tester feedback:

  • The category you selected and the description you typed
  • The display name you've set in the app
  • Basic app info: app version and platform (Android/Windows)
  • Usage and progress statistics: your level and XP, gold balance, login streak, total app opens, selected theme and avatar, and counts of your tasks, reminders, hyperfocuses, behaviors, and achievements
  • Whether a few optional developer-only settings are enabled (for example, Minimalist Mode and Dev Mode status)

This information is included to help the developer understand and reproduce reported issues — none of it includes the actual content of your tasks, notes, or journal entries. Before you submit feedback, the app shows you the exact information that will be sent (an expandable "Included with your feedback" section) so you can review it first. Submitting feedback is never automatic — it only happens if you actively fill out and submit that form.

Permissions

The app requests the following device permission:

  • Notifications — used only to deliver reminders you've set within the app. No notification content is sent anywhere outside your device.

Third-Party Services

Raven Tasks does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services. The only third-party service involved is Google Forms, used solely to collect feedback you choose to submit through the app's Feedback form, as described above. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that data once submitted.

Children's Privacy

Raven Tasks is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Raven Tasks adds new functionality in the future that changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account or sync feature), this policy will be updated accordingly before that functionality is released, and the effective date above will change.
