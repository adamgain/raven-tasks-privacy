Privacy Policy for Raven Tasks

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Overview

Raven Tasks is a task and productivity app developed by Adam Gain. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data Collection

Raven Tasks does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no network or internet functionality of any kind.

Everything you enter into the app — tasks, reminders, mood logs, hyperfocus notes, journal entries, and any other content — is stored entirely on your own device, in a local database. It never leaves your device, and the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

The app requests the following device permission:

  • Notifications — used only to deliver reminders you've set within the app. No notification content is sent anywhere outside your device.

Third-Party Services

Raven Tasks does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services.

Children's Privacy

Raven Tasks is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the fact that it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If Raven Tasks adds new functionality in the future that changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account or sync feature), this policy will be updated accordingly before that functionality is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact
